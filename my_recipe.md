\# Easy Vanilla Cake



\## Ingredients

\- 2 cups flour

\- 1 cup sugar

\- 1 cup milk

\- 2 eggs

\- 1 tbsp vanilla extract

\- 1 tbsp baking powder



\## Instructions

1\. Preheat oven to 180°C

2\. Mix dry ingredients in a bowl

3\. Add wet ingredients and mix well

4\. Pour into greased pan

5\. Bake for 30 minutes

6\. Let cool before serving
